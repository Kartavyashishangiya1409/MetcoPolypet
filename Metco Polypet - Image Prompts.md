# Metco Polypet — 3D Image Prompts

Use with any image generator (Midjourney, DALL·E, Firefly, Ideogram, etc.). Paste the **Master style** in front of every prompt below.

Each prompt is labelled with the slot ID it fills on the website. Drag the finished PNG onto the matching slot.

---

## Master style (paste before every prompt)

> Photorealistic 3D product render, studio quality, made in the style of high-end Cinema 4D / Octane / KeyShot. **Fully transparent background (PNG with alpha channel), no backdrop, no floor, no shadow plane, no gradient, no text, no logos, no watermark.** Object isolated and centred with generous empty margin. PET plastic rendered physically accurately: glass-like transmission, index of refraction 1.57, crisp specular highlights, subtle internal reflections, sharp thread edges, injection-moulded surface finish. Soft three-point studio lighting with a thin rim light so edges read against both light and dark website backgrounds. Ultra sharp, 8K detail, true-to-scale proportions of a real PET preform (test-tube shape: threaded neck finish on top, flat support/neck ring below the threads, long tapered body, rounded closed bottom). Not illustrated, not cartoon, not flat, not stylised, not vector.

**Output settings:** PNG, transparent background, 2048 × 2560 px (4:5 portrait) unless stated. If your tool cannot export transparency, generate on pure #00FF00 green and remove it, or run the image through a background remover (remove.bg, Photoshop "Remove Background").

**Negative prompt (where supported):** background, floor, table, shadow, reflection plane, gradient, text, label, logo, watermark, cartoon, illustration, low poly, blurry, deformed threads, bottle, cap, liquid.

## Do not generate with AI

Use real material for these slots:

- **logo-a … logo-e** — the client's real customer logos.
- **team-1 … team-3** — real leadership portraits.
- **contact-map** — embedded Google Map of the plant.
- Factory, warehouse or lab photos of the **actual Metco plant**, if available. Real photos of their own facility build more trust than renders.
